## Market check

There is a real opening: the global leaders each serve one slice (groups, dinners or dating), charge in USD/EUR, and none is M-Pesa-native or built for church, corporate and dating circles under one trusted identity.

Organisers: Standard US$44.99/mo | Organiser fees are the money engine; [price hikes since 2024 caused organiser backlash](https://www.group.app/blog/meetup-alternatives/) |
| [Timeleft](https://en.lebonbon.fr/bordeaux/tops-unusual/welcome-to-timeleft-bordeaux-apph1-pmeet-new-people-and) | Personality-matched dinners with strangers | €12.99 trial, then €19.99/mo; [food and drinks paid on the day](https://help.timeleft.com/hc/en-150/articles/27949169792924-What-Events-and-Subscription-Plans-Does-Timeleft-Offer) | People pay for curated, low-effort IRL meetups; women-only and themed formats sell |
| [Bumble (incl. BFF)](https://subger.com/en/service/bumble-nordic) | Dating, friends, networking modes | Premium from €19.99/week (Nordic) | One profile, several modes, freemium limits on likes and visibility |

**Domain and name.** A search for beabuddy.com shows no active product using it; similar names exist (a [university mentoring scheme](https://lists.rwth-aachen.de/archives/list/bebuddy@lists.rwth-aachen.de/thread/X25J6BIUCO4RNH62RDQC25DDHVOTT7DO) called BeBuddy, a kids' toy brand). Confirm the domain at a registrar, then file a trademark with KIPI in classes 9, 42 and 45 before launch.

**Kenya compliance.** You cannot act as a data controller in Kenya [without registering with the ODPC](https://bowmanslaw.com/insights/kenya-data-protection-getting-ready-for-registration/) unless exempt. A dating feature processes sensitive data, so plan a Data Protection Impact Assessment and a named DPO from day one.

**Gaps you can own:** M-Pesa and local pricing; verified identity across every circle; church and corporate organiser tools; safe dating with group-first meetups; goals and dreams as the core of matching, not just looks or interests.

## Vision and positioning

Be a Buddy is a verified, goals-first community app where people find their people: for faith, work, fun, growth and love, online and in real life.

- **Tagline options:** "Find your people." · "Show up for each other." · "Every goal needs a buddy."
- **Core idea:** every profile carries a short list of goals and dreams (run a 10K, start a business, grow in faith, find a partner). Matching, groups and events are built around shared goals, so connections have a reason to last.
- **One identity, many Circles:** a person verifies once and switches between Circles (Friends, Faith, Work, Dating and more). Each Circle has its own profile visibility, so a church member never sees someone's dating profile.
- **Two sides:** Members (join, match, attend) and Hosts (churches, companies, clubs, venues, event organisers). Hosts bring the crowds; members bring the subscriptions.
- **Launch market:** Nairobi first, then Mombasa, Kisumu, Kampala, Kigali, Lagos and Accra, then the diaspora (UK, US, Gulf).
- **Brand feel:** warm, human, image-led; real photos of real meetups, no stock-photo crowds.

## Trust, safety and compliance

Safety is the product: an app that puts strangers in the same room must verify people, protect women and keep Circles apart.

**Verification ladder**

1. Phone OTP at sign-up (required for everyone).
2. Selfie liveness check (required to message anyone or RSVP to in-person events).
3. Government ID check via a KYC provider such as Smile ID (required for Spark, hosts, and any paid event).
4. Organisation verification for churches, companies and venues (registration certificate plus a call).

**Safety features**

- Women-only and men-only events and filters; hosts can require verified attendees only.
- Share-my-plan with a trusted contact, check-in timer, in-app SOS that shares live location.
- Block, report and "unmatch" in two taps; reported users frozen pending review.
- AI moderation of photos and messages (nudity, scams, harassment, money requests), with human review within 24 hours.
- Romance-scam guard: warn when someone asks for money, M-Pesa numbers or moves off-app fast.
- Ratings after every meetup (private to Be a Buddy), feeding a trust score.

**Age rules:** 18+ only at launch. A teens or youth-ministry space would need parental consent and different design; keep it out of v1.

**Compliance checklist (Kenya first)**

- [ ] Register as data controller (and processor if relevant) with the ODPC.
- [ ] Data Protection Impact Assessment covering dating, faith and location data.
- [ ] Appoint a Data Protection Officer.
- [ ] Consent screens per Circle; separate consent for sensitive data (faith, relationship intent).
- [ ] Privacy policy, terms, community guidelines, refund policy, safety centre page.
- [ ] Register the business with eCitizen BRS and get a KRA PIN; VAT registration once turnover requires it.
- [ ] M-Pesa Paybill or Till via Daraja API; PCI-compliant card processor.
- [ ] KIPI trademark filing for "Be a Buddy".
- [ ] Event-safety terms for hosts (liability, venue capacity, alcohol rules).

Get a Kenyan tech lawyer to review the terms and the DPIA before launch; this plan is not legal advice.

## Go-live and launch plan

Launch in Nairobi with hosts first, members second: one church, one company and five venues bring the first 1,000 members far faster than ads.

1. **Weeks 0–2: Foundations.** Secure beabuddy.com and social handles, register the company, ODPC and KIPI filings, M-Pesa Paybill, waitlist landing page with "Pick your Circle" signup.
   - Gate: 1,000 waitlist sign-ups.
2. **Weeks 2–10: Build v1 (web app + PWA).** Circles 1–8, verification, matching, events, M-Pesa, safety kit, admin and moderation console.
3. **Weeks 10–12: Closed beta.** 3 founding churches/ministries, 2 corporate pilots, 5 venue partners, 300 invited members. Free premium for founders.
   - Gate: 40% of beta members attend at least one meetup; no-show rate under 25%; zero unresolved safety reports.
4. **Weeks 12–14: Public launch in Nairobi.** Turn on paid plans, launch "Buddy Week" (7 days of free events across Circles), PR in Business Daily and tech blogs, campus ambassadors.
5. **Months 4–6: Native apps.** Ship iOS and Android (Expo), WhatsApp reminders, Swahili UI; open Mombasa, Kisumu and Nakuru.
   - Gate: 5,000 monthly active members, 8% paying.
6. **Months 6–12: East Africa and diaspora.** Kampala and Kigali, diaspora chapters in London and Dallas, wave-2 Circles, sponsored seasons.
7. **Year 2: West Africa and beyond.** Lagos and Accra, wave-3 Circles, live audio, Buddy Wrapped.

**Growth levers**

- Host-led growth: every church or company brings its whole community; give hosts a free first 3 months.
- Referral: give a buddy one free month, get one free month.
- Content: TikTok and Instagram stories from real meetups (with consent), "Strangers to Buddies" series.
- Ambassadors: paid campus and church ambassadors on a per-active-member bonus.
- Partnerships: gyms, cafés, coworking spaces, matatu-saturated malls for pop-up sign-up booths.

**Metrics to watch weekly:** sign-up to verified rate, meetups attended per member, no-show rate, 30-day retention, paid conversion per Circle, safety reports per 1,000 meetups.

You are the lead engineer building Be a Buddy (beabuddy.com), a verified, goals-first community app where people find their people across Circles: Friends, Faith, Work, Dating (Spark), Goals, Fitness, Buddy Table and Students.

House rules (non-negotiable):
- UK spelling in all UI copy and code comments.

- Messaging: Resend (email), Africa's Talking (SMS), WhatsApp Business API placeholder.
- AI: an LLM via server routes only, for match explanations, icebreakers, moderation pre-screening and host event drafts.
- Maps: Mapbox or Google Maps (placeholder {{MAPS_KEY}}).
- Analytics: PostHog. Errors: Sentry. Hosting: Vercel + Supabase.

## 2. Brand and design
- Warm, human, image-led. Real meetup photography; rounded cards; generous spacing.
- Palette tokens (placeholders until I confirm): {{BRAND_PRIMARY}}, {{BRAND_SECONDARY}}, {{BRAND_ACCENT}}; full light and dark themes.
